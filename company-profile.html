Who We Are

About
Our Company

Nexora Group was founded in 2006 in London with a singular purpose: to help ambitious organizations navigate complexity and emerge stronger. What began as a boutique strategy consultancy has grown into a full-service transformation partner trusted by Fortune 500 companies, fast-growth startups, and public institutions alike.

Our integrated model spans advisory, technology implementation, and organizational capability-building — enabling us to deliver end-to-end solutions that create measurable, lasting impact.

Our History
2006
Founded in London
Nexora launched as a three-person strategy consultancy, securing its first FTSE 100 client within six months.
2011
Technology Practice Launched
Established a dedicated digital transformation practice, expanding to offices in New York and Singapore.
2017
Acquisition of DataFlow Inc.
Strategic acquisition of data analytics pioneer DataFlow, adding 140 specialists and proprietary IP to the group.
2024

The Challenge

Rebuilding an Institution from the Inside Out

Meridian Financial Group — a tier-one European bank with $180B in assets — faced a critical inflection point. Legacy core banking infrastructure, built over three decades, was throttling growth, increasing operational risk, and eroding the customer experience that defined their market position.

Nexora was engaged to lead a full-scope transformation: decommissioning 14 legacy systems, migrating 3.2 million customer accounts, and deploying a cloud-native core banking platform — all without a single day of customer-facing downtime.

Our Approach

Three-Phase Delivery

PHASE 01 — DISCOVER

14-week diagnostic mapping 280+ processes, system interdependencies, and data quality across all business units.

PHASE 02 — BUILD

Parallel-track development with weekly sprint reviews, risk dashboards, and a dedicated Nexora war room embedded on-site.

PHASE 03 — LAUNCH

Big-bang cutover executed over a single weekend with zero customer-facing downtime and full regulatory sign-off.
