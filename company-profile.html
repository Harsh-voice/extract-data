Who We Are

About
Our Company

Nexora Group was founded in 2006 in London with a singular purpose: to help ambitious organizations navigate complexity and emerge stronger. What began as a boutique strategy consultancy has grown into a full-service transformation partner trusted by Fortune 500 companies, fast-growth startups, and public institutions alike.

Our integrated model spans advisory, technology implementation, and organizational capability-building — enabling end-to-end solutions that create measurable, lasting impact.

The Challenge

Rebuilding an Institution from the Inside Out

Meridian Financial Group — a tier-one European bank with $180B in assets — faced a critical inflection point. Legacy core banking infrastructure built over three decades was throttling growth, increasing operational risk, and eroding the customer experience.

Nexora was engaged to lead full-scope transformation: decommissioning 14 legacy systems, migrating 3.2 million accounts, and deploying a cloud-native core banking platform — with zero customer-facing downtime.

Our Approach

Three-Phase Delivery

PHASE 01 — DISCOVER

14-week diagnostic mapping 280+ processes and system interdependencies across all business units.

PHASE 02 — BUILD

Parallel-track development with weekly sprints and a dedicated Nexora war room embedded on-site.

PHASE 03 — LAUNCH

Big-bang cutover executed over one weekend with zero downtime and full regulatory sign-off.
